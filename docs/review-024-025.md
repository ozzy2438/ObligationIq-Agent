**Source:** [Clause 167(1)(b)(i)–(iv), physical PDF page 120 / printed page 119](https://www.esc.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/Energy%20Retail%20Code%20of%20Practice%20%28version%206%29_2.pdf#page=120).

**Trigger:** life support registration details or required communications change.

**Candidate requirement:** keep the life support customer/resident register current, including:

- the date supply is needed for the life support equipment;
